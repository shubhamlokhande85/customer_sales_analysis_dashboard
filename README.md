## Customer Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

### 📌 Project Overview


This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior and sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard provides insights into monthly sales trends, customer demographics, gender-wise purchase distribution, and city-wise sales performance.


This project was completed as part of my Internship at Swynex Technologies, where I gained practical experience in data analysis, data visualization, dashboard development, and business intelligence.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze monthly customer purchase trends.

Identify high-value customer age groups.

Compare purchase contributions by gender.

Analyze city-wise purchasing performance.

Monitor important business KPIs.

Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization.

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.

DAX – Calculated measures and KPI creation.

Data Visualization – Interactive charts, cards, and slicers.

### 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

Visualization	Purpose

Monthly Sales Trends	Analyze monthly purchase patterns.

Purchase by Age Group	Identify high-purchasing age segments.

Purchase by City	Compare city-wise purchase amounts using a treemap.

Purchase by Gender	Compare male and female purchase contributions using a donut chart.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Customers

Total Purchase

Average Purchase

Average Feedback

Interactive Filters

Year Slicer

Month Slicer

These slicers allow users to filter the dashboard dynamically and explore sales performance for selected periods.

### 💡 Key Business Insights


Monthly Sales Trends: Purchase amounts fluctuate over time, highlighting peak and low-performing months.

Customer Demographics: The 45–60 age group contributes the highest total purchase amount.

Gender Analysis: Male and female customers have similar purchase contributions.

City Performance: City-level analysis helps identify leading markets and potential growth opportunities.

### 🚀 Business Recommendations

Target the 45–60 Age Group: Develop targeted marketing campaigns and personalized offers.

Improve Sales Planning: Plan promotional activities during low-performing months.

Expand City-Level Opportunities: Strengthen leading markets and explore opportunities in lower-performing cities.

Maintain Customer Satisfaction: Monitor customer feedback and address negative experiences.
